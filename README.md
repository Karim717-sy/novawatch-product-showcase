# NovaWatch Product Showcase

Create a modern, professional and fully responsive product marketing website for a fictional smartwatch brand called NovaWatch.

TECHNOLOGY:

Use HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Keep the project simple, clean and easy to maintain.

DESIGN:

- Premium technology brand aesthetic.

- Elegant dark navy and white color palette with electric blue accents.

- Modern typography, smooth animations and professional layouts.

- Responsive on desktop, tablet and mobile.

- Use high-quality free smartwatch product images.

PAGES AND FEATURES:

1. HOMEPAGE (index.html)

- Professional navigation bar with logo NovaWatch.

- Hero section with a large smartwatch photograph.

- Main headline: "Your Life. Smarter."

- Short product description.

- Call-to-action buttons: "Discover NovaWatch" and "Explore Features".

- Product benefits and key specifications.

- Feature cards linking to their individual detail pages.

- Footer with navigation and contact links.

2. FEATURE PAGES

Create separate detailed pages for:

- Health Monitoring: heart rate, sleep tracking and activity monitoring.

- Smart Connectivity: notifications, calls and smartphone connectivity.

- Battery Performance: long battery life and fast charging.

Each page must include:

- A relevant product image.

- Detailed explanations.

- Benefits and specifications.

- Navigation back to the homepage.

3. LANDING PAGE

Create a dedicated marketing landing page encouraging visitors to discover NovaWatch and register their interest.

Include a clear headline, product benefits and a call-to-action form.

IMPORTANT:

- All navigation links must work.

- All pages must have consistent branding.

- Include a clear contact link in the navigation or footer.

- Use real free image assets from reliable sources.

- Avoid placeholder text and broken images.

- Make the website visually polished and functional.
